About This Page

Who is on which site, from each worker's record. A verified punch keeps the record up to date; the office corrects it with Move… when a punch could not.

Status

Today · 3 Days
The last punch or bot line placed the worker at the site on the record, and how long ago. Not always a verified punch: a punch with weak or no GPS is placed at the record site too.
Today · HF-XXX-000
Seen at another site, named by its code. The record may be wrong: check, then Move… if so.
Today · No Site
Punched, but the punch was placed at no site: it matched no pinned site, or its GPS was too weak to place it.
No Report
Nothing in the last 7 days. Office staff carry no status.
